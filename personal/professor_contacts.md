## 📧 Cold Email Best Practices

**Timing:**
- Send Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11 AM professor's local time
- Avoid Monday mornings (inbox overflow) and Friday afternoons

**Subject line rules:**
- Include "Prospective intern" or "Internship inquiry"
- Mention timeframe (Fall 2026)
- Add one credential (age + key skill)
- Keep under 50 characters if possible
- NEVER use "URGENT" or all caps

**Email body:**
- **First sentence:** Who you are + why them specifically (not a mass email)
- **Second paragraph:** Your proof (GitHub link + specific technical details)
- **Third paragraph:** What you want (internship, timeframe, funding status)
- **Final paragraph:** Call to action (CV attached, request for guidance)
- **Total length:** 150–200 words max

**Attachments:**
- CV as PDF (named: Arustamov_CV_2026.pdf)
- (Optional) 1-page project summary with screenshot of GitHub
- Nothing else — no ZIP files, no massive portfolios

**Follow-up:**
- No response after 7 days → polite follow-up
- Still no response after 14 days → move on, don't stalk
- Template follow-up: "Dear Professor [Name], I wanted to follow up on my email from [date] regarding a Fall 2026 internship opportunity. I remain very interested in contributing to your lab's research. Please let me know if you need any additional information."
