# Cover Letter Templates (Markdown)

## Standard Template

```markdown
[NAME]
[PHONE] | [EMAIL] | [LINKEDIN]
[DATE]

[Hiring Manager Name / Hiring Team]
[Company Name]
[Company Address / Location]

## Re: [Role Title] — [Company Name]

**Dear [Hiring Manager Name],**

[HOOK — 1-2 sentences: Why this company and role specifically. Reference a recent company achievement, product, or value that resonates with you.]

[PROOF — 2-3 paragraphs: Relevant experience with metrics. Match 2-3 key requirements from the job description with specific examples from your background.]

[FIT — 1 paragraph: Why you + this role = success. Connect your strengths to their needs. Show forward-looking enthusiasm.]

[CALL TO ACTION — 1 sentence: Express readiness for interview, availability.]

**Best regards,**
[NAME]
```

## Structure Rules

1. **Hook** — Personalized, not generic. Show you researched the company.
2. **Proof** — Every claim backed by example + metric.
3. **Fit** — Bridge their needs → your strengths.
4. **Forward-looking** — What you'll do for them, not just what you've done.
5. **Tone** — Confident but humble. Professional but personable.

## Output Format

Save as `output/cover_[company]_[role].md`
