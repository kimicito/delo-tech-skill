# CV Templates (Markdown)

## Standard Template

```markdown
# [NAME]
[LOCATION] | [PHONE] | [EMAIL] | [LINKEDIN]

## Summary
[2-3 sentences: who you are, what you do, what you're looking for]

## Skills
**Core:** [skill_1], [skill_2], [skill_3]
**Tools:** [tool_1], [tool_2], [tool_3]
**Languages:** [language_1] (level), [language_2] (level)

## Experience

### [ROLE] | [COMPANY] | [DATES]
[LOCATION / REMOTE]
- [Achievement with metric]
- [Achievement with metric]
- [Achievement with metric]

### [ROLE] | [COMPANY] | [DATES]
[LOCATION / REMOTE]
- [Achievement with metric]
- [Achievement with metric]

## Education
**[DEGREE]** in [FIELD] — [INSTITUTION] ([YEAR])
[Thesis / Relevant courses]

## Projects
**[PROJECT_NAME]** — [TECH_STACK] ([DATES])
[Description + outcome]

## Certifications
- **[CERT_NAME]** — [ISSUER] ([YEAR])

## Additional
- [Open source contributions / Publications / Awards]
```

## Tailoring Rules

1. **Reorder skills** — Most relevant to the job first
2. **Quantify bullets** — "Increased X by Y%" not "Worked on X"
3. **Mirror keywords** — Use exact terms from job description
4. **Trim fat** — Remove irrelevant experience, keep to 1-2 pages
5. **Lead with impact** — Strongest bullets first in each role

## Output Format

Save as `output/cv_[company]_[role].md`
