# Interview Preparation

## Likely Questions (Generate 5)

### Technical
1. **"Tell me about a challenging project you worked on."**
   - Structure: Context → Challenge → Action → Result (CAR)
   - Include metrics and your specific contribution

2. **"How do you handle [specific technical scenario from job description]?"**
   - Reference the exact tech stack/tools from the job
   - Show problem-solving process, not just answer

### Behavioral
3. **"Describe a time you had a conflict with a teammate."**
   - Use STAR: Situation → Task → Action → Result
   - Focus on resolution, not blame

4. **"Why do you want to leave your current role?"**
   - Never trash current employer
   - Frame as growth-seeking, not escaping

### Career
5. **"Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"**
   - Align with company trajectory
   - Show ambition but realism

## Questions to Ask Interviewer (Generate 3)

1. **Team structure:** "How is the team structured? Who would I work with daily?"
2. **Success metrics:** "What does success look like in this role in the first 6 months?"
3. **Growth:** "What opportunities for growth exist here?"
