# Job Evaluation Framework

## Scoring Rubric (0-10 scale)

### Skills Match (weight: 30%)
- 9-10: 80%+ of required skills matched, plus nice-to-haves
- 7-8: 60-80% of required skills matched
- 5-6: 40-60% matched, willing to learn rest
- 3-4: <40% matched, large gap
- 0-2: Fundamental mismatch

### Experience Match (weight: 25%)
- 9-10: Exact years + domain match
- 7-8: Close years or adjacent domain
- 5-6: Transferable skills, some gap
- 3-4: Significant experience gap
- 0-2: Junior applying for senior (or vice versa)

### Culture Fit (weight: 15%)
- 9-10: Values aligned, team structure matches preference
- 7-8: Mostly aligned, minor differences
- 5-6: Some alignment, some concerns
- 3-4: Potential culture clash
- 0-2: Fundamental mismatch

### Career Growth (weight: 15%)
- 9-10: Clear growth path, mentorship, learning opportunities
- 7-8: Good growth potential
- 5-6: Moderate growth
- 3-4: Static role
- 0-2: Dead end

### Compensation (weight: 15%)
- 9-10: Above expectations + benefits
- 7-8: At expectations
- 5-6: Slightly below but negotiable
- 3-4: Significantly below
- 0-2: Unrealistic gap

## Total Score Formula
```
Total = (Skills × 0.30) + (Experience × 0.25) + (Culture × 0.15) + (Growth × 0.15) + (Compensation × 0.15)
```

## Action Thresholds
- **8.0+**: Strong match — apply immediately
- **6.5-8.0**: Good match — apply with tailored CV
- **5.0-6.5**: Marginal — apply only if interested
- **<5.0**: Skip

## Red Flags (Auto-decline)
- Unrealistic requirements (5+ years for junior role)
- Vague job description (no specific responsibilities)
- No salary range listed + unwilling to disclose
- High turnover signals (re-posted frequently)
- Toxic culture indicators (reviews, Glassdoor)
