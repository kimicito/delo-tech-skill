## What is Storewars?

**Storewars** is a complex business simulation game designed for FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) companies. It simulates a real consumer-facing business environment where participants learn to operate in a modern retail/manufacturing ecosystem.

> "We create a virtual business world for you to learn. So you don't lose in a real one."

**Scale:** Used by leading consumer-facing companies in **70+ countries**, **25,000+ participants**, **1,000+ programs**, available in **8 languages**.

---

## Core Concept

- **Format:** Business simulation game / experiential learning
- **Participants:** 15-24 people (mid and upper-level executives)
- **Teams:** 5 teams — 2 **retail** companies + 3 **manufacturing** companies
- **Duration:** Virtual period of 1-1.5 years (compressed into workshop sessions — typically 1-2 days)
- **Goal:** Capture maximum market share and increase profitability
- **Format options:** In-person (Storewars) or online (Storewars WEB)

---

## How It Works (Program Stages)

1. **P&L Analysis** — Understand current financial state
2. **Market Opportunities** — Analyze market data
3. **Strategy & Planning** — Develop company strategy
4. **Trade Activities & Negotiations** — Negotiate between retailers and manufacturers
5. **Business Decisions in Simulation Software** — Input decisions into the simulator
6. **Results** — See market response and financial outcomes

Teams interact and cooperate with each other. The success of their **cooperation** has a major impact on the results.

## Key Advantages

- **Business simulation experience** — Practical experience vs. classroom training (people remember 80% of what they experience vs. 20% of what they hear)
- **Real-like learning environment** — Replicates complex interplay between manufacturers, retailers, consumers
- **Helicopter view** — Escape "silo view", see entire company
- **Risk-free environment** — Safely test decisions with no risk to real company
- **Immediate results** — Business year compressed into 1-2 days

## E-Retail Workshop (Separate Program)

**One-day workshop** focused on digital transformation:

### Phases:
1. **Presentation by moderators/invited speakers** — Big picture of e-commerce and e-retail tools
2. **Development session** — 5 groups (4-5 people each), work with imaginary company profiles, transform them to e-level using special templates and e-commerce tools
3. **E-strategy presentation** — Understanding e-tools, consistency, cost of new strategy, creative solutions

### Learning Outcomes:
- Understanding consumers/customers
- Marketing and promotion
- Establishing points-of-sale & handling payment mechanisms
- Arranging distribution and logistics
- Establishing appropriate KPIs and feedback loop
- Providing organization resources (people and assets)

## Assessment

**Storewars** is a **competitor/complement** to Logistoria's existing offerings:
- **Logistoria** focuses on **supply chain simulation** (KADENA, Beer Game, Crossdock) — physical flow, logistics operations
- **Storewars** focuses on **FMCG commercial negotiations** (retailer-manufacturer dynamics, trade marketing, pricing) — commercial relationships
- **Potential overlap:** Both target corporate training, universities, supply chain professionals
- **Differentiation:** Logistoria = operational supply chain; Storewars = commercial/trade negotiations
- **Opportunity:** Could inspire a Logistoria "Trade Marketing" or "Retail Negotiation" module, or partnership/licensing
- **E-Retail** program is notably different — digital transformation focus, could complement Logistoria's e-commerce module
